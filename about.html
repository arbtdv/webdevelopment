About Me

My Story

I grew up in a family where food was always a big deal. My grandmother taught me how to cook and she is the reason I fell in love with it. She never used a recipe, she just knew what to do, and I always wanted to be like that.

Now I work as a private chef, which means I cook for people in their own homes. It is a lot of work but I like how personal it is. I get to learn what each person likes and make something just for them.

What I Value

The most important thing to me is using fresh ingredients and not overcomplicating a dish. I also believe that the way you treat people in your kitchen matters just as much as the food you serve.
