About My Cooking

My name is Addy and I am a private chef. I started cooking when I was young and it became something I really love to do. This website is for my culinary portfolio, where I share the food I make and the experiences that have shaped me as a chef.

Cooking is not just a job for me, it is a way to connect with people. Every dish I make is a chance to show someone I care, and that is the part I enjoy the most.

Good food is the foundation of genuine happiness.

Addy — September 20, 2026
