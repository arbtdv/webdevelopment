My Reflection

My Experience and Goals

Throughout my time in college, I have learned a lot about myself and my professional goals. I am interested in marketing, fashion, and creative work. I hope to find a career where I can combine creativity with business.

My work experience has taught me how important communication and adaptability are. Working in customer service, management, and content creation has helped me develop skills that I can use in my future career.

This web development course has helped me understand how websites are built. Before taking this class, I had very little experience with HTML and CSS. Although coding can be frustrating sometimes, I have enjoyed learning how to design my own website.

In the future, I hope to continue learning about marketing and digital design. I want to work in a creative environment where I can grow professionally. Creating this website has helped me understand how to present my skills and experiences online.

Addilyn Betts | September 10, 2026
